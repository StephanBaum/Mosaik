Liquid glass: unlock and lock

While the password is checked (simulated 1.1 s) a light band sweeps the button. On success a thick glass lens grows out of the point you clicked. The vault is the real DOM, revealed through a clip-path polygon that follows the lens outline exactly, so it is clickable from the moment it appears; outside the lens you still click the unlock screen. WebGL paints only the glass band (refraction, six-tap dispersion, Fresnel film, specular, rim line). Lock (top right of the vault) closes the lens into the point you clicked. Any password other than mosaic shows the drain and jitter.

alex@example.com

Liquid glass. The lens grows with a hard push and an irregular, gently wobbling outline. Its bevel bends the vault beneath it, with spectral dispersion along the slope, while the unlock screen ends cleanly at the rim; refraction fades out before the bevel's inner edge, where the glass meets the live vault without a seam. Vault elements fade in just ahead of the rim. On Lock the clicked button dissolves at once, the rim closes over the vault and the glass thins out as it collapses into the click point.

Notes
How it is built, and what the app port needs

    
    
      
  • DOM first. The unlock screen is underneath, the vault on top. The vault is shown through clip-path: polygon() with 120 vertices on the lens outline. Clip-path also clips hit testing, so clicks inside the lens reach the vault and clicks outside reach the unlock screen, during the whole transition. Nothing is swapped at the end: the clip is simply removed.
    • 
      
  • One outline, two implementations. Radius curve, irregular shape and wobble are sums of sines (no noise), written once in GLSL for the band and once in JS for the polygon, so both compute the identical shape.
    • 
      
  • The band. The WebGL canvas has pointer-events: none and is transparent except for the bevel band and the rim line. Inside the band it samples a snapshot of the vault (never the unlock screen, so the Unlock button is never repeated inside the glass); the refraction offset falls to zero before the band's inner edge, where the band fades into the live DOM. When the transition ends the canvas is empty.
    • 
      
  • Snapshots. No html2canvas, no foreignObject. A small painter walks the DOM and draws backgrounds, borders, text runs, SVG icons (Path2D) and the foil canvases (clipped to their corner radius) into a 2D canvas. The vault is one sprite per animated element, recomposed each frame with the same poses the DOM has.
    • 
      
  • Review hook. ?t=0..1&dir=unlock|lock&ox=&oy=&theme=dark freezes one frame with the lens origin at (ox, oy) in stage px; ?te=-0.1 / ?te=0.3 freezes relative to the end.
    • 
      
  • Fallbacks. Without WebGL2: the clip-path lens alone. Reduce motion (toggle or OS setting): a 250 ms crossfade, no glass.
    •

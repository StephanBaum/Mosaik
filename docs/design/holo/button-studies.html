v5 textures

v5 with the texture swapped. Here each texture has a fixed size in screen pixels, so large buttons show more repetitions instead of a finer, invisible pattern, and the texture drives the thin film and the surface normal strongly enough to read. Palette, sheen and rim are v5. The first row is plain foil for comparison. Each row shows 88, 56, 36 and 28 px; rows are still until you hover them.

Fix jaggies renders at the device pixel ratio (capped at 2) and box-filters the stripes over each pixel's footprint, so detail finer than a pixel averages out instead of aliasing. Turn it off to see the original rendering. Small-size texture boost widens the pattern on buttons under 56 px, to at least 6 device pixels per period at 36 px and below, so it stays visible; it changes only the pattern scale, not colour or bump.

One fixed WebGL2 canvas paints the buttons on demand. The reference heroes animate; everything else is painted once and repaints only on hover, focus, press or scroll. With reduced motion on, nothing animates. Without WebGL2 the buttons fall back to a plain solid fill.

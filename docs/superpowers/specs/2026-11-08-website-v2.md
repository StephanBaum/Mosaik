# Website v2: pages, funnel, imagery

Status: plan for the build agent. Branch `feat/web`, base `fbb5460`. Replaces the single-page concept in `2026-11-07-landing-page-design.md` where the two conflict. Its tech rules still hold: build-time React, vanilla-TS islands, zero third-party requests, CSP and `checkOrigins`.

Owner direction this plan answers:
- Multiple pages, not one long scroll.
- A real lead magnet and funnel.
- Imagery that is part of the layout, not a "text block next to an image".
- More images in one consistent style, not pink and pale.
- A dark page and the holo button for the primary CTA only.
- Meaning of the art: something that is yours breaks into a pixel mosaic that nobody else can read.

Rejected and not coming back: the holo tile grid, rainbow, glitch, neon halftone blobs, the barn painting (it leaves the site), decipher shaders, single-page scroll.

Notation: text in `[brackets]` is for the builder and never appears on a page. Pages carry no internal notes, no "pre-release" banners and no TODOs. Placeholders that render are only `{name}`, `{domain}`, `{PRICE}` and `{TEAM_PRICE}`.

> **Update 2026-11-08 (owner decisions, binding over the rest of this plan):**
> - **No password generation, input or checking anywhere on the website**, however local. The generator page, its WASM plan and every link to it are dropped. The only form is the email-capture card.
> - **Lead magnets:** the switch kit at `/switch/` and a two-minute multiple-choice self-check at `/check/` (§2.1, §5.5).
> - **Visual direction "Linear calm + holo hero":** no illustrations anywhere (the AI art set and patterns P2–P6, P9 are retired). The product is the imagery: real app screenshots (mock data, 2x, dark) in precise frames, close-up crops, and HTML mockups built from the app's tokens where no screen exists. The Home hero is a live holo-foil plane drawn with the app's own `foil()` shader behind the app window. Everywhere else: near-black ground, 1 px grid and rules, soft holo glows behind product shots, restrained colour, subtle reveals.

### 1.4 Routing and the static build

The existing Vite build already renders `/`, `/de/` and `/404.html` through `scripts/prerender.ts`. Generalize it:

1. **`src/pages/routes.ts`** is the single route table: `{ id, en: "/security/", de: "/de/sicherheit/", component, index: boolean, islands: IslandId[], csp?: CspOverride }`. `prerender.ts` loses its own `PAGES` constant and imports the table. Each route renders twice (EN, DE) to `<path>/index.html`. The dev middleware's `route()` looks up the same table. 404 stays one bilingual page.
2. **Head per page:** title, description, canonical, `hreflang` en/de/x-default, and OG image (one per page from §4). Keys follow `web.<pageId>.meta.title`.
3. **i18n:** split the `web` catalog into one file per page (`catalog/en/web/home.ts`, `security.ts` …), merged into `web`. The coverage test stays: every key a page renders exists in both languages. New copy from §5 goes in verbatim.
4. **Islands:** keep one entry, `main.ts`. It scans `[data-island]` and dynamic-imports only those islands: `holo`, `menu`, `downloads`, `assemble` (§3 P6), `generator`, `capture`. Pages without islands load no extra JS. The generator island and its WASM are fetched only on `/password-generator/`.
5. **CSP per route:**
   - The default stays as today.
   - The generator page adds `'wasm-unsafe-eval'` to `script-src`, and only there.
   - Pages that render the capture form get `form-action 'self'` once capture is enabled.
   - Both the meta CSP and `_headers` (path blocks) are generated from the route table.
   - `connect-src 'none'` stays on every page, including the generator. That is the proof that nothing leaves the tab.
6. **Also generated:** `sitemap.xml` (indexable routes, both languages, with `xhtml:link` alternates), `robots.txt` and `_redirects` (slash policy, `/de` → `/de/`).
7. `checkOrigins` keeps failing the build on any external origin. Compare-page source links are the exception: they are real external `<a href>`s. Add an explicit allow list of source URLs (from `marketing/comparison.md` §5) to the checker. Links are navigation, not requests. Keep the check strict for everything else.

## 2. The funnel

```
AWARENESS          INTEREST            CONSIDERATION          CONVERSION            ACTIVATION (in the app)
search, HN/Reddit  Home                Security               Download (OS first)   create vault + Emergency Kit
"password          Features            Compare hub/detail     ──► Next steps ──►    import (Switch guide)
 generator"  ──►   Generator page ──┐  Switch guide           Hosted signup         extension + first click-fill
"1password         (tool use)       │  Pricing, FAQ                                 choose sync
 alternative"                       │       ▲
                                    └► CAPTURE: "switch kit" by mail (double opt-in)
                                         └► 5-mail sequence ──► Download / Switch / Hosted
```

There are two exits, and both are first-class:
- **Ready now** (most visitors from Compare or Pricing): Download, then Next steps.
- **Not ready** (generator users, researchers): capture the email, run the sequence, then Download.

### 2.1 Lead magnets: the switch kit and the self-check

A password manager's website never generates, accepts or checks a password or any other secret, however local the code. Both magnets touch no secret.

1. **Switch kit** (`/switch/`, `/de/umzug/`): the full step-by-step migration guide on the page (1Password, Bitwarden, LastPass, KeePass, Enpass, Chrome and other browsers, anything else), the risky-minute warning, passkeys, the printable checklist and a short FAQ. The capture card offers the complete kit as a printable PDF plus the five-mail sequence (§2.3), in the coming-soon state until the endpoint exists.
2. **Self-check "How exposed are you?"** (`/check/`, `/de/check/`): seven multiple-choice questions (accounts, reuse, where passwords live, 2FA, what you would do after a leak, how new passwords are made, old accounts). Radio buttons only, no text inputs and no form element. A small TS island scores the answers in the page (0–15 points: up to 3 low, up to 8 mid, above high) and shows one of three plain results plus up to three next steps chosen by the answers, then the holo "Download free", the switch kit and the capture card. Nothing is stored (no storage, no URL state) and nothing is sent (`connect-src 'none'`, `form-action 'none'`). No statistics, no fear.

Tests: the built site has no `<input type="password">`, no generator route and no input other than the self-check's radios (plus the capture form's email field once capture is enabled).

### 2.2 Capture

**The offer** sits on the generator page after the first generate, on Switch, and at the end of Compare pages. It is the switch kit: a printable one-page checklist plus four short mails that walk through switching in order. Fields: email only, plus the list (hidden, from the page).

**Privacy rules:**
- The endpoint is ours, on our origin: `/api/v1/…`.
- A native HTML form POST that works with no JS. `form-action 'self'` stays on.
- Double opt-in. No third-party scripts, no tracking pixels, no per-person click redirects.

**Endpoint contract** (backend = owner and M9 item, §8.1):

| | |
|---|---|
| `POST /api/v1/subscribe` | `application/x-www-form-urlencoded`. Fields:<br>• `email` (required, ≤ 254, one `@`)<br>• `list` ∈ `switch-kit`, `teams`, `launch`<br>• `locale` ∈ `en`, `de`<br>• `src` (page id from the route table)<br>• `consent_v` (copy version, e.g. `2026-11-08`)<br>• `website` (honeypot, must be empty) |
| Response | Always `303 See Other` → `/thanks/` or `/de/danke/`, whether the address is new, known or a honeypot hit, so nothing can be enumerated. An invalid format → `303` to the same page with `?e=format`, which the page renders as an inline error. No JS needed. |
| Rate limits | 5 per hour per IP (in memory only, never stored), 3 per day per address. Over the limit → the same 303. |
| Confirm mail | One plain mail with a link `GET /api/v1/confirm?t=<token>`. The token is 256-bit random, stored as SHA-256, single use, valid 7 days. |
| `GET /api/v1/confirm` | Returns a small page with a **Confirm** button (POST), so link scanners cannot confirm by prefetching. The POST → `303 /confirmed/`. |
| Unsubscribe | Every mail has `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` (RFC 8058) plus a footer link to a POST-button page. Unsubscribe deletes the record. Only a salted hash stays as a suppression entry. |
| Stored | `email, list, locale, src, consent_v, created_at, confirmed_at, step`. No IP, no user agent, no opens, no clicks. Unconfirmed rows are deleted after 7 days. |
| Mail | Text plus simple HTML, no remote images, no tracking. SPF, DKIM and DMARC on the sending domain. Sent through the provider's SMTP or API (§8.1); the list lives in our database, not at the provider. |

**Coming-soon state** (until the endpoint is live): the build flag is `site.captureEnabled = false` in `src/pages/site.ts`. The form is then not rendered as a dead form that swallows addresses. In its place is an honest card: "Mail sign-up opens soon. The whole switch guide is already here." with a link to `/switch/` (copy in §5.6). With the flag on, the card turns into the form, with no other change.

**Form UI** (signup-flow rules): visible label, `type="email"`, `autocomplete="email"`, one field and one button. The privacy line sits directly under the button. The error appears inline and keeps the input. The success page explains: check your inbox, the link is valid 7 days, look in spam, here is how to fix a typo (resubmit).

### 2.3 Email sequence: "Switch kit" (5 mails)

- Trigger: confirmation.
- Goal: the app installed and the import done.
- Exit: unsubscribe, or a click on "I've switched" (a link to `/download/next/?m=done`; the click is counted in aggregate, not per person, and it does not stop the sequence automatically because we do not track. The mail says the remaining mails are short and can be ignored).
- Sent in the subscriber's locale, from a person's name. Each mail has one job and one link.

| # | When | Subject (EN / DE) | Job | Outline |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Right after confirming | Your switch kit / Dein Umzugspaket | Deliver | Link to the printable checklist (`/switch/#checklist`), three lines on what the next mails cover, the one download link. |
| 2 | Day 2 | Export without leaving a copy behind / Exportieren, ohne Kopien liegen zu lassen | Quick win, safety | Export files are unencrypted. Where each app puts them, how to delete them and empty the trash afterwards, why to do it on your own computer. Link: Switch guide section for their old app. |
| 3 | Day 4 | Two keys, and why we can't reset either / Zwei Schlüssel, und warum wir keinen zurücksetzen können | Trust, objection | Master password and Secret Key in plain words. The Emergency Kit: print it, write the password line by hand, store it. Honest: lose both and nobody can help. Link: `/security/`. |
| 4 | Day 7 | Your cloud folder, or ours / Dein Cloud-Ordner oder unserer | Core value | Free sync through Dropbox, iCloud Drive, OneDrive or Nextcloud, step by step. When hosted sync is worth it. Same lock either way. Link: `/pricing/`. |
| 5 | Day 11 | The one setting there isn't / Die eine Einstellung, die es nicht gibt | Differentiation, convert | No fill without a click, exact-address matching, no telemetry. "If you haven't installed yet, this is the link." Ends the sequence; says no more mails unless they join another list. Link: `/download/`. |

Teams list (`list=teams`, from `/teams/`): one confirmation mail with the team setup steps (team folder or hosted, safety codes, offboarding), then a personal reply from the owner. No automated sequence. Launch list (`list=launch`, used only if a platform isn't released yet): exactly one mail when it is.

### 2.4 Measurement without analytics scripts

Measure only what our own servers see. Never per person.

| Signal | Source | How |
|---|---|---|
| Page views per path and locale | Host access logs | Daily rollup job: path, status, referrer host (no full URL), count. Raw logs deleted after 24 h; no IPs in the rollup. Interim (static host only): the host's own aggregate counts if it has them without client scripts, otherwise wait for M9. |
| Download intent | `/dl/<platform>` same-origin redirect (M9) | Counts per platform and day, then 302 to the file. Until M9 the links go straight to the files and the count comes from the file host's logs. |
| Campaign source | `?ref=<campaign>` on inbound links, `?m=<mail-id>` in mails | Counted in the rollup; the same value for everyone (no per-person ids). |
| Capture | `/api/v1/subscribe` counters | `requests{list,src,locale}`, `confirmed{list}`, `unsubscribed{list}`, per day. |
| Activation proxy | Sync server (exists, M6) | New hosted accounts per day (the server already knows accounts). Extension store install counts from the store dashboards. The app has zero telemetry, so in-app activation is not measured, and we say so in the privacy policy. |

Weekly funnel sheet (owner): visits → generator uses (page views of `/password-generator/`; generation events are not counted, since that would need a request) → captures → confirms → `/dl` clicks → hosted signups. Experiments run one change at a time, comparing weeks. With small numbers they are directional only.

## 3. Image integration patterns

Rule: an image never sits in a column beside a text column of equal weight. Text and image always overlap, interlock or hand off to each other through cubes. All shapes snap to an 8 px "cube grid" so the CSS cubes match the rendered ones.

**P1. Stage with floating product cards.** The image is a large rounded stage (radius 32, 16:9 or 21:9). Real app shots (`/img/app-*`) float over its lower edge as tilted glass cards (radius 20, 1 px tinted border, deep shadow), overlapping out of the stage by 15–25 %. The headline sits on the page's dark ground above or left, never on the image's busy part.

```
 Eyebrow
 HEADLINE IN TWO LINES                     
 Sub line.   [ Download free ]  (holo)
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  gradient bg      ▪ ▪▫ ▪   ▫                 │
│        KEY ═══════▪▪▪▫▪ ▪  ▫  ▪              │
│                    ▪▫ ▪ ▫▪     ▫             │
│      ┌────────────────┐    ┌──────────┐      │
└──────┤  app-vault card├────┤ gen card ├──────┘
       │                │    └──────────┘
       └────────────────┘
```

**P2. Bleed band with overlapping text cards.** A full-bleed 21:9 image band. Two or three dark glass cards (`--ink-raised` at 88 % with backdrop blur; a solid fallback where blur is unsupported) overlap its bottom edge by about 120 px. They carry the section's points. The band's dominant colour becomes the section tint (P7).

```
══════════════════════════════════════════════════  full-bleed image
     vault door, cubes pulled out to the right  ▪▫▪ ▪
══════╦══════════════╦══════════════╦═════════════
      ║ 2022         ║ 2026         ║
      ║ LastPass …   ║ Researchers… ║   cards overlap the band
      ╚══════════════╩══════════════╝
```

**P3. Bento interlock.** A 12-column grid of tiles. Image tiles (1:1, 4:5, 2:1) and text tiles alternate in an irregular pattern. The cubes in one image tile continue across the gutter into the neighbouring text tile as CSS cubes (P4 at small scale). App-shot tiles crop to one UI detail (the fill popup, the 2FA code, the generator).

```
┌────────────┬──────┬──────────────────┐
│ IMG passkey│ text │  APP SHOT crop   │
│   ▪▫▪ ▪ ▫ ─┼─▪ ▫  │  (generator)     │
├──────┬─────┴──────┼───────┬──────────┤
│ text │ IMG phone  │ text  │ text     │
│      │            │       │          │
└──────┴────────────┴───────┴──────────┘
```

**P4. Cube trail connector.** The dissolve direction of an image continues as a trail of cubes. The trail crosses the section gap and ends at the next section's CTA or eyebrow. It is drawn as a static SVG file per transition (`/img/trail-<page>-<n>.svg`), loaded as `<img>`; no inline styles under the CSP. Cube colours are sampled from the source image. Cubes get smaller and fewer along the path (20 → 4 px). It is decorative: `alt=""`, hidden from assistive tech.

```
  [image ▪▫▪ ▪ ▫]
               ▪  ▫
                  ▪   ▫
                     ▪    ·
  ───────────────────────── section gap
                        ▪  Eyebrow: Pricing
                           HEADLINE …
```

**P5. Pixel-stepped mask.** The image is clipped by a `clip-path: polygon()` (in the stylesheet) whose edge steps in 32 px squares, like a mosaic biting into the page. Use it on one edge only, the side the object dissolves towards. Text sits in the bitten-out space.

```
┌────────────────────────┐
│  IMAGE                 └──┐
│        object            └──┐    Text in the
│                    ┌───────┘    stepped space
│                ┌───┘
└────────────────┘
```

**P6. Scroll assembly.** As the section enters, the object assembles from scattered cubes. A canvas island (`assemble`) samples the image into cells and moves them from scattered positions to their places over 900 ms, then crossfades to the real image. It plays once, not scrubbed back and forth. With reduced motion, no WebGL or no JS, the static image shows. Use it twice on the site at most: the Security hero (the padlock) and the Switch hero (the puzzle pieces).

**P7. Colour-field tint.** Each section takes its image's dominant hue as a low radial glow over the ink: 10 % at the image, fading to 0 within 60 vh. It also tints card borders (`--tint` at 28 %) and eyebrow text (`--tint-text`, checked at 4.5:1). Pages set `data-tint="cobalt|tangerine|teal|violet|yellow|coral|mint"`, mapped in CSS. This ties image and UI together without putting text on pictures.

**P8. Sticky stage with scrolling steps.** A rounded image stage, sticky on the left (desktop ≥ 1024 px). Three step cards scroll past on the right, and the stage crossfades to each step's image (200 ms) as its card crosses the middle. Below 1024 px each step card gets its image as a P1 mini stage. One sticky region per page at most.

```
┌───────────────┐   01 Two keys, both yours.
│               │      text …
│  IMG step 1   │
│  (sticky)     │   02 You decide where it syncs.
│               │      text …
└───────────────┘   03 It notices when something's off.
```

**P9. Cut-out object chips.** Alpha cut-outs of the objects (background removed) at 160–240 px sit in a card's corner, breaking out of the card edge by about a third. Cubes spill outside the card. Used on Pricing cards, FAQ group headers and the capture card.

Where each pattern is used is in §5. Use P1 on every page hero, and limit each page to four patterns.

## 4. Images

### 4.1 Palette and style

The current set is soft lavender and peach. v2 keeps the style (one object, glossy voxels, studio light, empty gradient) and pushes the colour. The backgrounds are saturated two-stop gradients. The cubes always mix 4–5 colours from the cube palette, with one colour leading per image.

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Electric cobalt | `#2B4BFF` | backgrounds, cubes |
| Ultramarine deep | `#1A1F7A` | background dark stop |
| Warm yellow | `#FFC21A` | backgrounds, cubes |
| Tangerine | `#FF7A1A` | backgrounds, cubes |
| Coral | `#FF5A4E` | cubes, accents |
| Deep teal | `#0B6E6E` | backgrounds |
| Mint | `#4DE3B0` | cubes, backgrounds light stop |
| Violet | `#7A3CFF` | accents, cubes |
| Aubergine | `#2A1240` | richer dark stop |
| Sky | `#5EC8FF` | light stop |

Pink is allowed only as a small share of cubes, never as a background.

**STYLE** (append to every prompt verbatim):
> bright minimal surreal 3D render, high-end studio product photography, soft global illumination, a single object floating in an empty seamless gradient space, the object dissolving into hundreds of small glossy colourful cubes of varied sizes (2 to 30 mm), crisp cube edges, faint soft contact shadow below, generous negative space, saturated clean colours, high contrast, no text, no logos, no letters, no people, no haze, no dust, no lens flare, no glitch, no rainbow, no neon, no pastel pink wash, photorealistic materials

**Negative prompt** (where the model supports one): `pastel, washed out, pale pink, lavender haze, fog, dust particles, glitch, chromatic aberration, rainbow gradient, neon glow, text, watermark, logo, multiple objects, clutter, people`

Deliverables per image: master PNG at least 3200 px on the long side; AVIF and WebP at the widths the layout needs (like the current `name-WIDTH.ext` scheme); for P9 chips, an alpha cut-out (WebP with alpha). File names: `art-<id>-<width>.<ext>`.

## 6. Design system

**Palette (dark UI).** Replaces the barn-derived values in `base.css`:

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `--ink` | `#0C0B14` | page ground, a violet-tinted near-black |
| `--ink-raised` | `#14131F` | cards |
| `--ink-deep` | `#08070E` | footer, tool card well |
| `--paper` | `#F5F2EA` | text |
| `--paper-2` | `#C9C6D6` | secondary text (≥ 7:1 on ink) |
| `--paper-3` | `#9693A8` | captions (≥ 4.5:1 on ink, check on raised) |
| `--rule` | `rgb(230 226 255 / 0.10)` | hairlines |
| `--tint-*` | cobalt `#2B4BFF`, tangerine `#FF7A1A`, teal `#18A3A3`, violet `#8A5CFF`, yellow `#FFC21A`, coral `#FF5A4E`, mint `#4DE3B0` | per-page `data-tint`, glows, borders |
| `--tint-text-*` | lighter versions that pass 4.5:1 on `--ink` (e.g. cobalt → `#8FA2FF`) | eyebrows, links |

Add these to `packages/design-tokens` as a web palette (`webColors`) with contrast tests, as the existing token tests do. Styling must go through design tokens.

**Bleed.**
- A section's glow: a radial gradient of `--tint` at 10 % alpha behind the image's position.
- Card borders: 1 px `--tint` at 28 %.
- Focus rings stay paper (do not tint them, for contrast).
- Image stages get a 1 px inner highlight and a coloured shadow (`0 40px 120px -40px` tint at 45 %), so the picture glows onto the page.
- Never put text directly on image backgrounds, except P1 hero headlines placed on a stage's empty negative space at ≥ 4.5:1. Verify per image; if it fails, the headline moves above the stage.

**Type.**
- DM Sans variable (self-hosted, as now). Display at 64–104 px, weight 600, tracking −0.035 em, line height 0.95. H2 at 40–56 px, weight 600. Body 18 px, line height 1.55, measure ≤ 64 ch.
- IBM Plex Mono for eyebrows (12–13 px, uppercase, +0.08 em, `--tint-text`), numbers (01, 02), and the generator read-out (28–40 px).

**Shape.** Radius: stages 32, cards 20, inputs and chips 12, buttons pill. Everything else snaps to an 8 px grid. The P5 step size is 32 px.

**Motion.**
- Reveal: opacity and 16 px rise over 600 ms, ease-enter, once.
- P6 assembly: 900 ms, once per page view.
- P8 crossfade: 200 ms.
- Hover: 120 ms.
- No parallax on text, no scroll-jacking, no autoplay video.
- With `prefers-reduced-motion`, everything is static: no reveals and no assembly, and images show in final state.
- The existing hero pixel-sort island (`src/hero`) and its shaders are retired with the barn. Keep `fx/` only if P6 reuses its stage; otherwise delete.

**Holo button.**
- Only the page's primary CTA, once per viewport, never in the header, never on cards, and not in the generator tool (it competes with the tool's own Copy button).
- The label is always a verb + outcome ("Download free", "Download for Windows").
- The no-WebGL fallback stays as ADR-0014 defines it.

**Performance budget per page:**
- LCP image ≤ 180 KB AVIF at 1600 w.
- CSS ≤ 40 KB gzip in total.
- JS islands ≤ 15 KB gzip each, except generator WASM (§2.1).
- No layout shift from images: width and height are always set.

## 7. Build plan

Run one agent at a time, with checks scoped to `apps/web` (`pnpm --filter @mosaic/web typecheck`, `lint`, `test`), and run the full gate once before each commit.

1. **Art first** (what the owner sees first, before any code):
   - Generate #1 `key`, #6 `lock`, #12 `card`, #14 `puzzle` and #16 `dice` with §4 prompts, 4 variants each.

5. **Compare (hub + 2), Switch, Teams, FAQ, Check a download:** new copy from §5. Matrix from `comparison.md`, with the build guard on `n/v`.

   - ADR (move the strength module into generator; WASM `getrandom` backend).

## 8. Owner decisions (each with a default)

1. **Email backend and provider.**
   - Default: a small separate axum service (`crates/site-api`), not inside the sync server, to keep marketing code off the security-critical server. Behind the same origin at `/api/v1/`, with SQLite storage.
   - Sending through an EU transactional provider with a DPA (e.g. Mailjet or Scaleway Transactional Email), API or SMTP only; the list stays in our database.
   - Built in M9. Until then the coming-soon state.
2. **Legal pages.**
   - Default: the owner supplies the Impressum data (name, address, contact; required for a German site) and approves a privacy policy covering: host logs (24 h, aggregated), the mailing list (double opt-in, consent log, deletion), no cookies, no analytics, the mail provider as processor, and the app's zero telemetry.
   - The production build refuses to deploy without them.
3. **Domain and name.** Default: keep `{name}` = "Mosaic" and `{domain}` = `mosaic.example` as placeholders, wired through the catalog, so a rename is one change. The domain is needed before the mail sender is set up (SPF, DKIM, DMARC).
4. **WASM generator.**
   - Default: yes, do it in v2 (step 7). It is the owner's earlier wish and the strongest lead magnet.
   - Cost: one ADR, one new dependency (`wasm-bindgen`), a small crate move, and about two to three agent sessions.
   - Fallback if declined: ship v2 without the generator page; Switch becomes the capture page; the tool follows later.
5. **Public docs links.** Default: the Security "Read it" block and the Compare "open source" bullet wait for the license decision. Until then the block is hidden and the bullet stays.
6. **Prices.** Default: `{PRICE}` and `{TEAM_PRICE}` stay placeholders. The Pricing and Teams pages show them as written; the build warns (not fails) while they're unset in dev, and fails in production.
7. **Image generation.** Default: the owner's current text-to-image tool, with the prompts in §4.3. About 18 × 4 variants plus cut-outs; the owner approves the first five before the rest are made.
8. **More compare pages** (Proton Pass, KeePassXC, Enpass, LastPass alternative). Default: v2.1, from the same template once v2 is live, each with its cells re-verified on the day.
